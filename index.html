A little about me

Who I am

An engineer who likes the whole span of a product.

I'm a software engineer in İzmir who's never picked a side of the stack. My favourite projects are the boring-in-the-best-way ones: tools real people open every day, like the Laravel CRM I built and still keep running in production.

My thesis was on real-time emotion detection with AI — a good reminder that the interesting problems usually sit where people and machines meet.

Away from the editor I'm slowly collecting languages: comfortable in English, getting there in Korean, and chipping away at German and Japanese. It's the same itch as programming, really — learning a new grammar and seeing what you can say with it.

I like small teams, clear problems, and shipping things that outlive the sprint they were built in. But also am pretty adaptive.
